## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🦺 SafeVision AI

**AI-powered workplace safety and fatigue detection system**

A YOLOv8-based Computer Vision system designed for workplace PPE compliance and fatigue detection.

**Highlights:**

* 🥈 **2nd place out of 38 teams at ARIIF 2026**
* Trained and evaluated **four model iterations (v1–v4)**
* Designed custom augmentation strategies, including:

  * **SLRDA** — a self-built augmentation approach
  * **SR-inspired CLAHE** preprocessing pipeline
* Developed an **uncertainty-aware confidence scoring engine**
* Worked on the system from **dataset preparation → training → evaluation → inference**
* Developed a business case around the Egyptian workplace-safety context
* Incorporated **Egyptian Labour Law No. 14 of 2025** and **CAPMAS injury statistics**
* Performed competitor analysis against **AvidBeam Technologies**
* Submitted as part of the **Egypt Cities Hackathon (ECH)**
* Research direction targeting potential publication in **IEEE Access / MDPI Sensors**

**Focus:** `Computer Vision` `YOLOv8` `Object Detection` `Deep Learning` `Image Processing` `Model Evaluation`
